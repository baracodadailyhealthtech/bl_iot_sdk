JTAG Connection

1.1 Connect to the On-board T-HEAD Debugger.

Connect the following 4 GPIOs to the JTAG signals input IO on the J3 connector of the BL70xL DVK v1.0 :

  • IO0(J3.10) - TMS(J3.12)

  • IO1(J3.09) - TDI(J3.11)

  • IO2(J3.13) - TCK(J3.15)

  • IO7(J3.18) - TDO(J3.16)
